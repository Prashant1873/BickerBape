## Design Specificity Verdict: Authored (High Product Coherence)

BickerBape demonstrates genuine product-specific craft built around "The Quant Observatory" metaphor. It avoids generic SaaS templates by enforcing tabular figures (`tnum`), zero synthetic CAGRs, and fiduciary track record seasoning penalties. The primary design tension lies in the split visual identity between the institutional Royal Ultramarine Screener and the Cyberpunk Neon SimSim Time Machine.

## Priority Issues

1. **[P1] Color Contrast Failures on Financial Metric Indicators**
   - *Impact*: Emerald gain text (`#36B37E`) on light card surfaces yields a 2.66:1 contrast ratio, failing the WCAG AA 4.5:1 floor.
   - *Fix*: Darken text-level positive returns to `#00875A` or `#059669` while preserving `#36B37E` for chart strokes and badge fills.
   - *Command*: `/impeccable colorize`

2. **[P1] Modal & Drawer Keyboard Focus Leaks**
   - *Impact*: Opening the deep-dive drawer or comparison modal does not move or trap keyboard focus, allowing focus to escape into background DOM.
   - *Fix*: Trap focus within active dialogs, place initial focus on header action, and restore focus on dismiss.
   - *Command*: `/impeccable harden`

3. **[P1] Sub-44px Touch Targets on Mobile Controls**
   - *Impact*: 18 utility buttons (drawer close, tray minimize/clear, sidebar collapse) measure under 36px, causing mobile mis-taps.
   - *Fix*: Expand touch envelopes to 44x44px.
   - *Command*: `/impeccable adapt`

4. **[P1] Global 0.01ms Motion Kill in Reduced-Motion Mode**
   - *Impact*: Strips all transition cues abruptly, depriving motion-sensitive users of state change confirmation.
   - *Fix*: Replace with gentle opacity cross-fades without spatial translation.
   - *Command*: `/impeccable animate`

5. **[P2] Visual Identity Split Between Screener and SimSim**
   - *Impact*: Screener uses Royal Ultramarine (`#0052cc`) and soft slate, while SimSim uses high-saturation neon green (`#00F090`) on deep black (`#0B0F19`), creating a jarring aesthetic jump.
   - *Fix*: Harmonize SimSim's chrome with Fiscal Clarity tokens or formally document the dual-theme system.
   - *Command*: `/impeccable document`

6. **[P2] Screener Table Lacks Sticky First Column on Mobile**
   - *Impact*: Panning rightward across 8+ columns hides the scheme name, forcing repetitive back-and-forth scrolling.
   - *Fix*: Pin first column with `position: sticky; left: 0`.
   - *Command*: `/impeccable adapt`

7. **[P2] Undersized Micro-Labels (< 11px)**
   - *Impact*: 30+ labels use 9px and 10px text, causing visual strain.
   - *Fix*: Standardize on 11px/12px font size with medium/bold weights.
   - *Command*: `/impeccable typeset`
